Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open the site, GitHub processes your IP address and technical request data (date and time, requested file, browser type, referrer) in server log files to deliver the site and keep it secure. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is a reliable, secure website.

GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Maps (MapTiler)

Map and satellite imagery are loaded from MapTiler AG, Höfnergasse 7, 6300 Zug, Switzerland. To deliver the map tiles, your browser sends your IP address and technical request data to MapTiler. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is displaying an interactive map, which is the core function of this site. Switzerland has an EU adequacy decision. See the MapTiler Privacy Policy.

Local storage in your browser

Your choice of units (metric or imperial) is saved in your browser's local storage so it is kept on your next visit. This value never leaves your device. Storing it is necessary for the function you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time via your browser settings.

Everything else runs locally

Fonts, the map library, population data and place names are served from this website itself; no third-party font or script services are used. Location search, effect calculations and casualty estimates are computed in your browser and are not sent to us. Share links only contain the scenario parameters (weapon, yield, coordinates) and are only sent where you choose to share them.
